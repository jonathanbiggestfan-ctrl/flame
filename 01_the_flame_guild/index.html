F.L.A.M.E.

Federation of Labourers, Artisans, Mages and Engineers.

"Keep the F.L.A.M.E. burning!"

🔥 Campaign: The Right to Rest (and Respawn)

we are tired of being tired. Mages, smiths, engineers, and potion brewers are are working long hours without proper breaks. We're not asking for magic - just a moment to eat our stew in peace.

Our 3 Main Campaign Goals

  1. The 4-Hour Rule - Every worker gets a break every 4 hours. Even during dragon sieges.
  2. Universal Bedroll Access - Free, flameproof bedrolls for all guild-certified workers.
  3. Respawn leave - If you're ressurected due to unsafe working conditions, you get the day off. No arguments.

Slogans you'll see around town

  • "Breaks before Blades!"
  • "No Stew, no Spells!"
  • "Stop the Grind - Nap Time Now!
